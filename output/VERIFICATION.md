| Bite width | Lean | CoM lower than upright by | Lowest bottom point Z (at Y) | vs bridge top |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| min (18) | 7.3° | 0.49 | 2.60 (Y 5.6) | -1.10 |
| nom (19) | 2.8° | 0.17 | 3.67 (Y 6.7) | -0.03 |
| max (20) | 0.2° | 0.00 | 4.32 (Y 7.9) | +0.62 |

An 18-wide (min) bite on two rail edges 16 apart is unstable upright: it rolls until it wedges between the side walls, and its low bottom corner hangs into the gap between the rails, about 1 mm below the bridge top (v1: 1.8 mm below the 4.5 bridge). Nominal bites lean ~3° and stay flush; max bites stay upright. A leaning bite meets the bridge edge with one rounded R2 corner, which rolls it upright about its high-side rail rather than lifting it bodily, so this is not the self-locking climb above, but no rigid-upright model covers it. **Bench go/no-go #1 (sticky 18-wide bites, clip resting in the socket, all 8 cycled) is the real test** (OPEN_ISSUES #14).

## 9.12 Return-spring solid length (v1.1)

Solid length = (n_active + 3)·d with n_active = G·d⁴ / (8·D³·k) (closed, unground ends; G = 79.3 GPa music wire; 302 SS is ~13 % softer, so fewer coils and shorter). It must be ≤ 10 and below the 12.1 pressed length, or the spring coil-binds before the flange reaches its hard stop and the lift is lost.

| Spring config | wire / OD / k | Solid length | ≤ 10 and < pressed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | 0.4 / 6.0 / 0.13 | 5.6 | PASS |
| A | 0.4 / 6.0 / 0.2 | 4.1 | PASS |

Over the BOM range for the spring-B return spring (k 0.12 → 0.15 N/mm):

| OD | wire | solid length at k 0.12 / 0.15 | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.5 | 0.4 | 7.6 / 6.3 | OK |
| 5.5 | 0.45 | 13.2 / 10.8 | **coil-binds** (≥ pressed length) |
| 6.0 | 0.4 | 6.0 / 5.1 | OK |
| 6.0 | 0.45 | 10.3 / 8.5 | **over 10** |
| 6.5 | 0.4 | 4.9 / 4.2 | OK |
| 6.5 | 0.45 | 8.2 / 6.9 | OK |

**0.4 wire meets the rule everywhere in the range.** Out of range: OD 5.5 with 0.45 wire reaches 13.2 (coil-binds); OD 6.0 with 0.45 wire reaches 10.3. So the "0.45 max" wire is only safe on a larger OD or a stiffer spring - buy 0.4.

## 9.13 Latch (§6.2, v1.1)

Socket clearance 0.1 below the clip, 0.3 above. Tooth bottom Z 32.80, notch floor Z 32.50 (clip centred).

| Clip position | Tooth engagement in the 1.0 notch | Tooth bottom → notch floor | Tongue strain when docking |
|---|---|---|---|
| on its socket floor (rest, gravity) | **0.60** | 0.40 | 1.41 % |
| centred | 0.70 | 0.30 | |
| floated up against the socket roof | 1.00 | **0.00** | 2.35 % |

Floated up, the tooth reaches the notch floor at exactly the moment the clip top reaches the socket roof: they touch, they never overlap (the clip-float poses in §9.1 confirm it), and the clip cannot rise further. Play in X 0.10; strain at 10.1 from the tongue root, PETG yield ~4 %. The tongue is 4.0 wide, in the -Y half only (OPEN_ISSUES #1).
